Inside the floor

everything a community needs
01

Challenges

Freestyle battles, song-pick challenges, 30-second solos. We post them, you show up. The point isn't winning — it's the reps.

02

Giveaways

Showcase tickets, merch, spot prizes. One tap to enter, winners drawn in the open. The house always gives back.

03

Announcements

Session moved? New challenge dropping? We post it here so nobody's left wondering. No rumors, no hearsay.

04

Playlists

The soundtrack of the floor. Members share what they warm up to, cool down to, and everything in between — so no one moves alone.

House rules

short, and we mean them
01

Nobody ranks you here

No skill leaderboards, no level-ups, no "who's best." You dance because you want to — that's the only metric.

02

Beginners belong up front

Everyone in this community had a first class. Ask questions, take the front spot, mess up loudly. That's how the floor works.

03

Show up messy

You don't need the moves perfected to post a challenge entry or share a playlist. Half-ready counts as ready.
